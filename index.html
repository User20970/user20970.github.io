About

Seven years of building — and proving it works.

I'm Xavier Dreschke, a software developer based in Pipestone, Minnesota. Since earning my Computer Science degree at the University of Minnesota in 2019, I've built my career at Atos as a remote developer on Guidewire InsuranceNow — an enterprise insurance platform — writing front-end and back-end Java solutions and carrying Jira tickets from requirement to release.

My path runs through both engineering and quality: five years of full-stack development plus two in automation and QA as an SDET. That dual background shows in how I work — I've written over a hundred JUnit tests to close coverage gaps, authored and peer-reviewed manual and automated test cases, and developed numerous code solutions to Jira tickets throughout my career. When something ships, it ships with coverage.

Beyond the codebase, I hold seven Salesforce certifications — including Administrator, Platform App Builder, and Platform Developer I — and I work comfortably across agile teams with business analysts and QA, maintain team documentation in Confluence, and fold AI tools like Microsoft Copilot into my workflow. I'm committed to continuous learning and improvement, especially in fast-paced environments.
